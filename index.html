Our vision

Simplifying business administration across Zambia.

At StartEasy Zambia, our goal is to bridge the gap between formal compliance requirements and the realities of how SMEs actually operate — so entrepreneurs can spend less time on paperwork and more time building their businesses.

This is an opportunity for individuals who believe in entrepreneurship, innovation, teamwork, and turning ideas into reality. If that describes you, keep reading.

Three open positions

The roles we are recruiting for.

Each role is designed for someone who wants to build, not just work. Read the responsibilities below and apply for the one that fits you best.

1 Position one

Co-founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

Preferred: Lusaka-based Female Strategic Leadership & Business Development
Ideal candidate A female good at public-facing work, with leadership ability, initiative, sound judgment, and a strong commitment to building a business.

Key responsibilities

  • Implement the startup's vision, mission, and business strategy while leading StartEasy's business planning and operations.
  • Identify business opportunities, partnerships, and growth strategies.
  • Drive the startup toward sustainability and long-term success.
2 Position two

Co-founder & Marketing Officer

Preferred: Kitwe / Lusaka-based Female Marketing, Branding & Customer Acquisition
Ideal candidate A creative, proactive female with strong communication skills, marketing knowledge, and an interest in business growth.

Key responsibilities

  • Develop and implement marketing and promotional strategies.
  • Build brand awareness and manage marketing communications.
  • Generate leads and support customer acquisition and retention.
  • Explore digital marketing, partnerships, and other growth opportunities.
3 Position three

Client Advisor

Preferred: young Zambian professionals / graduates Client Relations & Administrative Support
Ideal candidate A client-focused individual ready to support administrative service needs, build strong relationships, and deliver a positive client experience across PACRA, ZRA, NAPSA, NHIMA and other StartEasy desks.

Key responsibilities

  • Support clients with administrative service needs across the StartEasy desks.
  • Build and maintain strong client relationships.
  • Deliver a positive, end-to-end client experience.
  • Coordinate filings and follow-ups with PACRA, ZRA, NAPSA, NHIMA and other authorities.
What we value

We look for people who build.

Growth mindset

The willingness to learn, adapt, and get better every week — not just at hiring time.

Comfort with uncertainty

The ability to work in an evolving startup environment, where the path is being drawn as we walk it.

Teamwork

Collaboration, honest communication, and a genuine interest in seeing others succeed.

Initiative

You notice what needs doing and you do it — without waiting for a task list.

Co-founder opportunity & working arrangements

The two co-founder roles include an ownership arrangement — equity in the business, formalised in a co-founder agreement, with defined responsibilities, decision rights, and time commitments to be agreed with the founding team.

The Client Advisor role is a client-facing position within the StartEasy service desk, with its own responsibilities and expectations, also to be discussed during the application process.

We welcome individuals who are ready to contribute, collaborate, learn, and grow with us as we build the business.
